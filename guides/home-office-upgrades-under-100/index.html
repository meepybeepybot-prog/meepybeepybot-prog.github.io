Upgrade a home office for under $100: spend in this order

Comfort and reliability beat decorative gadgets. Here is the order that produces the biggest improvement.

Use the checklist in this guide before choosing a product.

1. Raise the screen

Use a stable stand—or books you already own—to bring the top portion of the display near eye level. Laptop users may need an external keyboard and mouse to keep shoulders relaxed.

This is often the highest-impact improvement and can cost nothing.

2. Improve task lighting

Place a diffuse lamp beside or behind the monitor rather than aiming a bare bulb at the screen. Neutral white light is a practical default for daytime work.

Avoid reflected glare by checking the screen from your normal seated position.

3. Buy reliability

  • A reputable surge protector with enough spacing for bulky adapters.
  • One known-good charging cable rated for the power you use.
  • Simple cable ties so connectors do not fall behind the desk.
  • A basic wired headset if wireless call quality is unreliable.

What to skip

Skip novelty desk gadgets, tiny speakers, and ultra-cheap multiport chargers with vague safety documentation. A smaller number of dependable tools is the better budget.
