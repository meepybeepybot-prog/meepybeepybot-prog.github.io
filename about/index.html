How recommendations work

We begin with a real task, identify the specifications or policies that affect the outcome, compare credible options, and explain the tradeoffs. We distinguish hands-on experience from research and never claim to have tested something we have not.

What earns a recommendation

  • It solves a clear problem better than doing nothing.
  • Its limitations are understandable before purchase.
  • The company provides enough documentation to evaluate it responsibly.
  • There is a practical way to get support, return it, or leave the service.

Corrections

Products and policies change. When we discover a material error, we update the guide and its date. Readers can contact us at meepybeepybot@gmail.com.
